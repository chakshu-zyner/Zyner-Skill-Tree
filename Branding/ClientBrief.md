# Client Brief - Northstar

## Company / Product

Northstar is a B2B SaaS platform that helps finance teams manage and forecast company spending.

It connects company expenses, budgets, subscriptions, and financial data in one place, giving finance leaders a clearer view of where money is being spent and what is coming next.

## Problem They Solve

Growing companies often manage spending across spreadsheets, bank accounts, expense tools, and disconnected systems. This makes it difficult for finance teams to understand spending, identify unnecessary costs, and accurately forecast future expenses.

Northstar brings this information together and gives finance teams a clearer, more reliable view of company spending.

## ICP

Northstar primarily serves growing companies with 50–500 employees.

Key users and decision-makers include:

- CFOs
- Heads of Finance
- Finance Managers
- Founders

The audience is financially experienced and expects products they can rely on when making important business decisions.

## Competitors

- Ramp
- Brex
- Airbase
- Spendesk

The category is crowded, and many competitors use similar fintech and SaaS visual language.

## Brands They Like

The client likes brands such as:

- Stripe
- Linear
- Notion
- Mercury

They like brands that feel well-designed, simple, refined, and confident. They appreciate strong typography, thoughtful details, and identities that don't rely on unnecessary visual decoration.

## Style / Vibe

The client wants Northstar to feel like a modern financial product built for serious businesses.

They don't want the brand to feel like a traditional accounting company, but they also don't want it to look like a trendy consumer finance app.

They prefer a clean and intentional visual identity with a distinctive logo and a strong but controlled use of color.

The identity should feel credible enough for finance leaders while still being approachable for modern companies.

Avoid generic fintech imagery, excessive gradients, overly playful illustrations, and visual styles that make Northstar look like every other SaaS startup.

## What We Need

Create a complete brand identity for Northstar, including:

- Logo
- Color palette
- Typography system
- Supporting visual language
- Brand applications
- Brand guide

## Key Challenge

Northstar operates in a crowded financial technology market. The identity needs to stand out from competitors while still feeling appropriate and trustworthy for people making important financial decisions.

**How can we create a distinctive identity that feels right for Northstar and its audience?**
