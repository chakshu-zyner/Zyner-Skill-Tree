# Client Kickoff Call - Mingle

**Project:** Mingle  
**Project Type:** Product Launch Motion Graphic  

**Participants:**
- Maya — Founder of Mingle
- Alex — Project Manager
- Designer

---

### 00:00 - Introduction

**Alex:**  
Thanks for joining, Maya. To start, can you tell us a little about Mingle and what you're building?

**Maya:**  
Sure. Mingle is an app for finding people nearby who are into the same things as you.

Not dating, not networking. Just meeting people you actually have something in common with.

---

### 01:30 - Why Mingle?

**Alex:**  
What made you want to build it?

**Maya:**  
I think meeting people gets surprisingly difficult once you're out of school or university.

You can move to a new city, start a new job, or just realize that most of your friends aren't really interested in the things you're interested in.

You might love photography, play in a band, be really into climbing, whatever it is. Finding other people who are into the same thing isn't always easy.

That's where the idea came from.

---

### 03:30 — How It Works

**Alex:**  
How does Mingle work?

**Maya:**  
You create a profile and choose the things you're interested in.

You can add quite specific interests too. It's not just "music" or "sports."

Then Mingle shows you people nearby who share those interests.

You can look through their profiles, find something you have in common, and start a conversation.

---

### 05:30 - Target Audience

**Alex:**  
Who are you mainly building this for?

**Maya:**  
Probably people in their twenties and thirties.

Especially people who have moved somewhere new or feel like their social circle hasn't really kept up with their interests.

But honestly, it could be anyone who wants to meet new people.

We don't want it to feel like an app only for a particular type of person.

---

### 07:00 - The Main Problem

**Alex:**  
What's the main problem you want the video to communicate?

**Maya:**  
That finding people you genuinely have something in common with can be difficult.

There are plenty of ways to meet people, but most of them start with location, work, school, or existing social circles.

We wanted to start with the thing you actually care about.

---

### 09:00 - What Makes It Different

**Alex:**  
There are already apps for meeting people. What makes Mingle different?

**Maya:**  
The interests come first.

We're not trying to randomly introduce you to hundreds of people.

If someone lives five minutes away but you have absolutely nothing in common, that's not particularly useful.

We'd rather help you find the person who's into the same weird band, plays the same sport, or spends their weekends doing the same thing you do.

---

### 11:00 - Tone

**Alex:**  
How do you want the video to feel?

**Maya:**  
Friendly and a little playful.

It shouldn't feel like a serious social networking product.

I'd like it to feel relatable. Maybe there's some humor in it.

The idea is that people should watch it and recognize that feeling of being really into something that nobody around you seems to care about.

---

### 13:00 - Visual Direction

**Alex:**  
Do you already have a visual style we need to follow?

**Maya:**  
We're still figuring that out.

We have a basic logo, but we haven't locked down a full visual system yet.

So we're pretty open to how the video looks.

We're happy for the motion piece to have its own visual personality as long as it still feels like Mingle.

---

### 14:30 - Product Visibility

**Alex:**  
How much of the actual app do you want to show?

**Maya:**  
It should be clear that Mingle is an app, obviously.

But I don't want a screen recording with arrows pointing at buttons.

The product is really about the people and the connection, so I'd rather the video tell that story and bring the app into it naturally.

---

### 16:00 - What to Avoid

**Alex:**  
Anything you definitely don't want?

**Maya:**  
Nothing too corporate.

And please don't make it feel like a dating app.

I also don't want the usual shots of people sitting in a coffee shop smiling at their phones.

If we're going to make a video, I'd rather it have an actual idea behind it.

---

### 17:30 - Deliverables

**Alex:**  
What are we making?

**Maya:**  
A short launch video, probably around 30 to 40 seconds.

The main version will be used on the website and social media.

And we'll need a voiceover.

---

### 19:00 - Final Thoughts

**Alex:**  
If someone watches the video and remembers one thing about Mingle, what should it be?

**Maya:**  
That there are probably people out there who are into the same things you are.

You just haven't found them yet.

**Alex:**  
Perfect. That's everything we need. Thanks, Maya.

**Maya:**  
Thanks. Looking forward to seeing what you come up with.
