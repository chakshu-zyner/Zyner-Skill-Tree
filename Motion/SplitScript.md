# Split - Client's Rough Script

Splitting expenses with friends can be complicated.

You go out for dinner, someone pays the bill, someone else pays for the taxi, and someone buys the drinks.

Then comes the difficult part.

You have to figure out who owes what.

You might use a calculator, go through your bank transactions, look at the receipt, and ask everyone what they ordered.

And if everyone paid for different things, it can get even more confusing.

Split makes it easier.

With Split, you can add your expenses, create a group, and add the people you're sharing with.

You can split the total equally or choose exactly what each person paid for.

Split calculates everything for you and shows who owes what.

You can then settle the expenses without having to do the calculations yourself.

Split also keeps track of your previous expenses and groups, so you can see what you've spent and who you've shared expenses with.

Whether you're having dinner with friends, going on a trip, sharing a house, or splitting everyday expenses, Split helps make managing money with other people easier.

No more calculations.

No more confusion.

Just split the bill and move on.
